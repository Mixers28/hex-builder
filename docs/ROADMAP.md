# Roadmap

The direction is a settlement builder: welcome citizens, support them, develop the town and extend its productive territory. Completion gates matter more than dates.

## 1. Playtest the first town loop

Implemented: housing and immigration, food consumption, automatic staffing and work pause, population research milestones, foragers, farms, replanting foresters, deep mines, save migration, and original construction-card artwork.

**Gate:** a player can grow from four pioneers to fourteen citizens, unlock all three technologies, maintain food, regrow timber, mine deeper and reload the same town. Test shortages and scarce workers as well as the successful path. Core checks alone do not prove the interface is comfortable.

## 2. Make the town feel inhabited

Refine citizen movement and workplace feedback, construction presentation, building silhouettes and ambient sound. Extend the original art set after reviewing the first cottage, farm and forester illustrations. Decide whether to move the older industrial buildings and carriers toward the same rustic visual direction.

**Gate:** players can identify homes, food, logging, planting and mining from the world view and understand why a workplace is waiting.

## 3. Deepen one settlement need

Possible next additions are household groups, explicit workplace assignments, a market distributing food, or agricultural seasons/fertility. These are not implemented. Choose one after testing whether the current food and housing loop creates useful decisions.

**Gate:** the selected need creates a visible planning choice, has a recovery path and survives saving without losing citizens or goods.

## 4. Improve logistics where playtests justify it

Candidates include inbound ore delivery, local stores, transport assignments and multiple depots. Define reservation, dispatch, arrival, cancellation, full destinations and blocked routes before adding another stock owner.

**Gate:** longer routes have understandable costs and interruption cannot duplicate or discard goods.

## 5. Validate devices and larger towns

Run an actual iOS export on a chosen physical device. Check touch selection, gestures, overlays, safe areas, lifecycle saves, memory and frame time with a developed town. Measure before expanding citizen models or terrain detail.

**Gate:** the complete town loop is comfortable and reliable on the target device. Desktop Play Mode is insufficient evidence.

## Scope decisions left open

Arbitrary tunnels require a different terrain system. Endless worlds require simulation/render/save scaling. Multiplayer requires authority and synchronization. Combat, taxation, trading and offline production require separate gameplay decisions. None is part of the current slice.

Preserve migration coverage when changing persistent state. Keep manual resource and food recovery available while refining balance.
