ALPHA HEX - FIRST FRONTIER
Windows playtest 0.1.0

A small, early single-player settlement-building demo. Welcome new citizens,
assign workers, discover stone beyond the fog, and reinforce a watchtower.
The longer game is still being developed. Armies, animals, rival villages and
boats are future plans; they are not in this demo.

START PLAYING

1. Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All.
2. Open the extracted AlphaHex folder.
3. Double-click AlphaHex.exe.

Keep AlphaHex.pck beside AlphaHex.exe. No Godot installation, account, internet
connection or administrator installation is needed to play.

Target: Windows 10/11, 64-bit Intel or AMD PC, mouse and keyboard, and a graphics
driver supporting OpenGL 3.3. This is an unsigned preview, so Windows may show
an unknown-publisher warning. A real Windows launch/performance test is still
pending; the export and the packed game have been checked on the developer's Mac.

Maximize the window for more space. The interface scales with the window.

YOUR FIRST VILLAGE

Plot coordinates appear in the right-hand inspector after you click a hex.

1. Build [B] a Cottage at (0,1), then a Forager lodge at (-1,1).
   Choose the building, click its plot, then click the inspector's Build button.
   Open Workers [T] and give the lodge one worker.
2. Build a Woodcutter at (-1,0), an Iron mine at (1,0), and a Foundry at (1,-1).
   Give each one worker. Goods reach your stores when their carts arrive.
3. Build another Cottage, for example at (0,-1), and keep food supplied.
   Homes fill gradually; assign each new worker yourself. Keep at least
   6 timber and 1 metal in reserve for another resource claim.
4. At six citizens, research Frontier surveying [R]. Claim (0,2) and connect a
   Watchtower through the cottage at (0,1). The tower reveals more land.
5. Discover stone at (0,5), research Stoneworking, then Quarrying tools at
   eight citizens. Claim (0,3), (0,4) and (0,5), road the first two, then build
   and staff a Stone quarry on (0,5).
6. At ten citizens, research Frontier masonry. Use delivered stone to reinforce
   your Watchtower and see further into the frontier.

You can continue growing after this milestone. The map and content are limited;
there is no victory screen. More workers speed up work, but crews still wait
for their deliveries. Trees and mineral deposits run out. Manual gathering on
owned plots can help you recover; it does not refill exhausted resources.

CONTROLS

Left click                 Select a plot
B / T / R                  Build / Workers / Research
1 / 2 / 3                  Inspect / Claim / Road
Enter                      Apply the selected action
WASD or arrow keys         Pan
Middle-button drag         Pan
Q / E or right-button drag Orbit
Mouse wheel                Zoom
Home or 7                  Return to the village
Space                      Pause / resume
Top-bar 1x / 2x button     Change speed
F on grass or woodland     Emergency food (eight-second cooldown)
F5 / F9                    Save / load
M                          Settlement menu
Menu > Save & Exit         Save your village and close the game
Escape                     Close a panel

SAVES AND PROBLEMS

Choose Menu [M] > Save & Exit to save your current village and close the game.
It resumes automatically next time you launch. If saving fails, an error is
shown and the game stays open so you can retry.

The game also saves automatically every 30 seconds and on normal window closure
when autosave is enabled. It progresses only while running. Each friend's
computer keeps its own town.
Use Menu > Begin a new settlement to restart; the previous village is archived.

Windows saves: %APPDATA%\AlphaHexFrontier\
Main file: frontier.json; fallback: frontier.json.bak
Log: %APPDATA%\AlphaHexFrontier\logs\godot.log

If launching fails, share the exact error or the log with the developer.
Keep the EXE and PCK together and ensure the ZIP was fully extracted.
